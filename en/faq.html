FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is rented at La Grimouillière?

The rental is the little house — an independent home with its own garden and no facing view — located within the grounds of La Grimouillière.

How many guests can the house welcome?

Up to 6 guests.

What are the main living spaces?

On the ground floor, the dining room with fireplace is the common living space of the house. It is extended by a living room with a large table, ideal for reading, spending time together and remote working.

Is the house suitable for families?

Yes — the layout is particularly convenient for a couple with children, thanks to the adjoining room with two single beds upstairs.

Is the boudoir a bedroom?

The boudoir is on the ground floor. It is not a main bedroom, but a flexible extra room with a TV that can be used as an additional sleeping space when needed.

How many bathrooms and WCs are there?

One bathroom upstairs, plus two fully independent WCs: one on the ground floor and one upstairs.

Can I work remotely from the little house?

Yes. The house benefits from high-speed Starlink Wi-Fi across the whole property, and the living room offers a large table particularly well suited to remote working.

Is the pool accessible?

Yes, the pool is accessible from 1pm to 6pm, from May to end of September.

Are pets allowed?

No, pets are not allowed.

How do I check exact availability?

Please send us a message with your travel dates.

Any questions?

Don't hesitate to get in touch.
