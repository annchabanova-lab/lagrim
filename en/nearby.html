NEARBY

Around the house

Cycling

Rolling roads between pastures and orchards, gentle or sporty loops. Pays d'Auge reveals itself by bike, at the pace of its sunken lanes.

Visits

Half-timbered manors, village markets, hidden Norman heritage. A short drive to Vimoutiers, Camembert, and the Haras du Pin.

Local producers

Cider farms, camembert makers and Pays d'Auge specialities. A generous terroir, right at your doorstep.

Wellness

In-home massages available on request. Details and availability upon enquiry.
