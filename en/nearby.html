NEARBY

Surroundings

Staying at the little house of La Grimouillière means enjoying a calm and refined setting in the heart of the Pays d'Auge — a land of orchards, hedgerow countryside, villages and local craftsmanship.

At 2h15 from Paris-Porte Maillot, the property is equally suited to a weekend escape and to a longer stay with family or loved ones.

THINGS TO SEE AND DO

  • Explore the rolling roads of the Pays d'Auge by bike or on foot
  • Discover the local villages and markets
  • Meet cider producers, Camembert makers and other Normandy specialities
  • Visit the studs, manor houses and small heritage sites of the region
  • Enjoy an in-house massage on request

A FEW IDEAS AROUND THE HOUSE

  • Camembert and its terroir
  • Vimoutiers
  • The Haras national du Pin
  • Village markets and local producers
  • The cider routes and Normandy orchards
