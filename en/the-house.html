THE HOUSE

The little house

A world of its own

Tucked away on a private estate, just next to the old cider press, La Grimouillière has been designed as a coherent universe. Each room tells its own story through carefully curated vintage furniture, textured wallpapers, and collected objects.

The farmhouse has been fully renovated while preserving its original character — exposed beams, stone walls, and the warmth of a home lived in and loved.
