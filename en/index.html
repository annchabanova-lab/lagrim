THE HOUSE

A suspended parenthesis

Only a few hours from Paris, the little house offers what the city no longer knows how to give: distance, silence, real countryside. Tucked away on a Pays d'Auge estate, just next to the old cider press, it has been designed as a world of its own. A coherent decor, each room in its own atmosphere, so that every stay feels like a suspended parenthesis. Come as a couple, with friends, with the children. Work remotely for a few days to stretch the weekend. Above all, find here what you came looking for: time.
